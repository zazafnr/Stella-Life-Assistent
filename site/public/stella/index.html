What is Stella

One assistant.
Plain sentences.

Stella is not another app full of menus. You tell her what is on your mind, she understands it, does it and shows you exactly what she did. Everything she does can be undone.

  1. 01

    Tell her

    Type, speak or send a photo. In the app, on the web, on your desktop or in Telegram.

  2. 02

    She handles it

    Reminders, appointments, lists, mail, plans. Quick things run without AI, harder things use a large language model.

  3. 03

    You stay in charge

    She asks first, tells you afterwards or just does it, per area. One tap undoes any action.

Features

Everything that

Goals

Progress without pressure.

“I want to run three times a week.” Stella counts along, checks in at the time you pick and finds room in your week. No streaks, no badges, no guilt.

Transparency

See everything she does.

One page lists every reminder, routine and automation. Switch any of them off, change the time, or undo what Stella just did.

The app shows your plan, how many AI messages and pro tasks you have used today and this month, and what the other plans add.

And the rest

Small things,
handled quietly.

  • Chat & voice

    Plain words in, done out.

    “Remind me in two hours to call Mum.” “Report due Friday.” Stella answers in a line and a card you can confirm or undo. Tap the mic to speak, she cleans up the transcript.

  • Briefing

    Your day on one page.

    What is due, tomorrow's lessons, your steps, goals and the mail that matters. In the morning Stella adds the weather and a few headlines.

  • Planner

    Plans your week for you.

    Deadlines and to-dos go in. Stella spreads the work across your free time, with breaks and a daily limit. Untick a block to leave it out.

  • Inbox

    Mail, sorted before you look.

    Connect Gmail or iCloud. Important mail rises to the top, promotions go to the bin, deadlines land in your planner. Replies are drafted and only sent when you say yes.

  • Calendar

    Month, week, day.

    All your appointments, deadlines and routines on one calendar. Drag to move, stretch to resize, undo with one tap. Subscribe to your other calendars with an ICS link.

  • Telegram

    Message her anywhere.

    Link Stella to Telegram once. Text, voice notes and photos work like in the app, and her answers arrive with Yes, No and Undo buttons.

  • Errands

    Reminders that know the shop.

    “Buy flowers after work.” Stella reminds you at the right time and shows florists nearby. Your exact location is never stored.

  • Restaurants

    Find, check, book.

    Ask for an Italian place Friday at eight. Stella lists what is open at that time, how far it is and where to book. If she cannot tell whether there is a table, she says so.

  • News

    Three headlines, not a feed.

    Pick topics and countries, or your favourite paper. A neutral one-line summary of each story in your morning briefing, with a link to the source.

  • Screen time

    Limits you set yourself.

    Name a goal such as “TikTok, 30 minutes”. On iPhone, Stella nudges you at half, 80 % and the limit, in the tone you chose. Only the goal and the threshold reach Stella, never which apps you use.

  • Routine

    Every Monday, forever.

    Repeating reminders carry on by themselves and pause or stop with one switch. Evening reminders arrive when you set them.

  • Memory

    She remembers, you edit.

    Stella keeps notes such as who is who and when you train. One page shows everything she knows about you, to view, change or delete.

  • Tone

    Nice, cheeky or cool.

    Choose how Stella writes to you. Every message, reminder and mail follows your tone, in your language.

  • Desktop

    Made for a big screen.

    The Windows app and the web version have their own layout: sidebar, week board, quick search with Ctrl+K and detail panels. Not a phone stretched wide.

  • Everywhere

    Eight languages, four places.

    iPhone, Windows desktop, any browser and Telegram. Sign in with a link in your email if you do not want a password.
