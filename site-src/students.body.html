School and apprenticeship

Your day,
as it really is.

  • Timetable

    Cancellations before you pack.

    Connect WebUntis or an ICS link. Stella shows lessons, cancellations and substitutions, and warns you the evening before.

  • Homework

    Photo in, solution out.

    Snap the worksheet or tell Stella the book page. She solves it, writes the answer as a notebook page in your own handwriting and reminds you the evening before.

  • Books

    The right page, found.

    “Maths page 84, exercises 3 to 5.” Stella opens the page of your textbook where the publisher allows it.

  • Exams

    A study plan that fits.

    Tell Stella what is on the test. She spreads study sessions over your free time, with breaks and a daily limit. Untick a block to leave it out.

University

Semester, courses,
grades.

  • Semester

    Courses and timetable.

    Add your semester and courses, subscribe to your university timetable and see lectures, labs and deadlines in one calendar.

  • Grades

    Your average, always current.

    Enter grades with their credit weight. Stella keeps the weighted average and shows how a coming exam could change it.

  • Flashcards

    Cards that come back.

    Make cards from your notes, an exam topic or a summary. Stella shows only what is due today and brings missed cards back sooner. No points, no streaks.

  • Notes from photos

    Board to summary.

    Photograph the board or your notebook, several pages at once. Stella reads them and writes an outline, key sentences and terms, and tells you what was unreadable. Turn it into flashcards or exam material.

And everything else

The full Stella,
underneath.

Chat and voice, calendar, planner, inbox with drafted replies, morning briefing, lists, Telegram and eight languages. Every action can be undone, and you decide per area whether Stella asks first, tells you afterwards or just does it.
