How it works

Say it the way
you would say it.

No forms, no menus. Write or speak in plain sentences. Stella understands, does it and shows you what she did.

Chat with Stella

Demo shown in English. The app itself speaks German today, with English on the way.

Your life, handled

Everything that
needs remembering.

Ask

Plain words in, done out.

“How much did I spend this week?” Stella answers in a line and a card, not a spreadsheet. Reminders, lists and plans work the same way.

Goals

Progress without pressure.

Say “I want to run three times a week”. Stella counts along, checks in at the time you pick and finds room in your week. No streaks, no badges.

Money

See where it goes.

An iPhone shortcut sends each card payment to Stella. She sorts them, spots subscriptions and warns you before a budget runs out. Only amount, merchant and card name are stored.

Transparency

See everything she does.

One page lists every reminder, routine and automation. Switch any of them off, change the time, or undo what Stella just did.

And the rest

Small things,
handled quietly.

  • Mail

    Sorted before you look.

    Important mail rises, promotions disappear. Replies are drafted for you and only sent when you say yes.

  • Errands

    Reminders that know the shop.

    “Buy flowers after work.” Stella reminds you at the right time and shows florists nearby. Your location is never stored.

  • Voice

    Hold, speak, done.

    Record a message instead of typing. Stella transcribes it and handles it like any other message.

  • Routine

    Every Monday, forever.

    Repeating reminders carry on by themselves, and pause or stop with one switch.

Control

You decide what
Stella may do.

  • Ask, tell or act.Per area, you choose whether Stella asks first, tells you afterwards or just does it.
  • Undo, always.Every action Stella takes can be taken back.
  • No tracking.Location only in the moment you ask for nearby shops, never stored.
  • Delete everything.One tap removes your account and all your data.
